Security+ Cost in 2026: Exam Price, Renewal and Jobs

By Prostutii Editorial Team • Last Verified: 2026-11-18
⚡Direct Answer / Summary
As of late 2026, a CompTIA Security+ exam voucher is reported at $439 in the US, or $579 with Retake Assurance, and the certification stays valid for three years. Renewal takes 50 continuing education units plus fees. Prices vary by country and change, so confirm on CompTIA’s store before you book.

Last verified: November 18, 2026. Prices are US list prices reported by third-party trackers in October and November 2026, because CompTIA’s store loads prices dynamically. Product details and policies were checked against CompTIA’s V7 and V8 pages and its continuing education FAQ.

Before you commit to Security+, you want three numbers: what it costs to sit the exam, what it costs to keep the certification, and what it can realistically do for your career. This guide gives you each one, shows where the figures come from, and tells you where to double-check them. It does not promise a job or a salary, because no certification can.

Key takeaways

How much does the Security+ exam cost?

In the US, the Security+ voucher was reported at $439 in late 2026, after CompTIA raised prices on June 1, 2026. A voucher with Retake Assurance was reported at $579, and bundles that add CertMaster training cost more. The table shows the reported US list prices.

OptionReported US priceWhat you get
Exam voucher$439One exam attempt
Voucher with Retake Assurance$579A first attempt plus a retake if you need it, on the same exam version
Practice and Exam Bundle with Retake$593CertMaster Practice plus a voucher with Retake Assurance
Learn and Exam Bundle with Retake$884CertMaster Learn plus a voucher with Retake Assurance
Complete Bundle with Retake$1,058CertMaster Learn, Labs and Practice plus a voucher with Retake Assurance

CompTIA’s own pages describe these bundles, and the V8 page lists the estimated study time for each CertMaster product: Perform 30 to 60 hours, Learn 25 to 40 hours, Practice 10 to 20 hours and Labs 15 to 25 hours. Prices differ by country, currency and tax, so check the CompTIA store for your region.

Pitfall: trusting an old price.
Older articles still quote $404 or $425, which were earlier prices. Read the price on CompTIA’s store on the day you buy, and be careful with discounted vouchers from resellers. Check that the seller is authorized and read the expiration terms. This guide does not endorse any seller.

Buying a voucher while two versions are live

SY0-801 launched on November 17, 2026, and English SY0-701 retires on June 11, 2027, according to CompTIA’s pages. Vouchers are described as valid for 12 months from purchase and for the exam version named in the product, so a voucher bought for one version should not be assumed to work for the other. Check the product page before you pay.

  • If you will test before June 11, 2027 and have studied for SY0-701, a SY0-701 voucher is fine, but schedule early so a reschedule does not push you past the retirement date.
  • If you are starting now, buy the voucher for the version you will sit. Use our exam decision calculator if you are unsure.
  • If you buy Retake Assurance, it is described as covering a retake on the same exam version, so a retake after a version retires may not be possible.

For the full comparison of the two versions, read the SY0-701 vs SY0-801 guide.

How renewal works and what it costs

According to CompTIA’s continuing education FAQ, certifications earned after December 31, 2010 are valid for three years from the certification date and are automatically enrolled in CompTIA’s Continuing Education (CE) program. To stay certified, you must complete the program’s requirements within the three-year window.

ItemWhat to knowSource
ValidityThree years from the certification dateCompTIA CE FAQ
EnrollmentAutomatic for certifications earned after 2010CompTIA CE FAQ
CEUs for Security+50 continuing education units in the cycleReported by multiple training providers; confirm on CompTIA
CE feeReported as $50 per year, $150 per cycle. Pay only when you are ready to upload CEUs.CompTIA CE FAQ (when to pay); amount reported by training providers
Paying fees aloneDoes not renew your certification. You must also submit approved CE activities.CompTIA CE FAQ
RefundsCE fee payments are not refundable, so pay only when you have enough CEUs.CompTIA CE FAQ

CompTIA also lists other renewal routes, such as passing a higher-level CompTIA exam, and training providers report a CertMaster CE renewal course. Rules and prices can change, so confirm in your CompTIA account, which shows your own expiration date.

Common ways to earn CEUs

  • Completing approved training or courses and attending approved events.
  • Earning another qualifying certification.
  • Documented work activity that CompTIA accepts.

CompTIA’s CE activities summary lists the approved types and how many units each earns. Check that an activity is approved and falls inside your three-year cycle before you rely on it.

What jobs does Security+ help you get?

Security+ is an entry-to-intermediate credential. It validates core security skills, and employers use it to screen for foundational knowledge. It is rarely the only thing a hiring manager looks at. CompTIA’s V7 page lists DoD 8140 work roles aligned with SY0-701, including these:

  • Cyber defense analyst and incident responder
  • Vulnerability analyst and security control assessor
  • System administrator and network specialist
  • Systems planner, IT project manager and information security manager
  • Secure software assessor

CompTIA notes the list continues with “many more.” Government and contractor jobs often reference DoD 8140, so ask the requirement owner which credentials and versions are approved for your role.

Titles worth searching in your own market

These are commonly searched entry-level titles, and whether a posting lists Security+ varies by employer and country: security analyst, SOC analyst, IT support or help desk with security duties, systems administrator, network administrator and cybersecurity technician.
