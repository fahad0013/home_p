CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 vs SY0-801: Which Exam Should You Take?

By Prostutii Editorial Team • Last Verified: 2026-11-18
⚡Direct Answer / Summary
Take SY0-701 if you can pass before it retires in English on June 11, 2027, and you have already started studying it. Take SY0-801 (launched November 17, 2026) if you are starting from scratch or testing after spring 2027. Both lead to the same Security+ certification, so the version mainly affects timing and study materials.

Last verified: November 18, 2026, against CompTIA’s official Security+ V7 and Security+ V8 pages. Always confirm dates and prices on CompTIA’s site before you book.

CompTIA Security+ now exists in two live versions at the same time. SY0-801 (Security+ V8) launched on November 17, 2026, while SY0-701 (Security+ V7) stays available in English until June 11, 2027. If you are mid-study, about to book, or just starting, the question is not which exam is “better.” It is which one you can pass sooner, with study material that matches it.

This guide gives you the official dates, a side-by-side comparison of the two blueprints, and a decision table for the situations candidates actually run into. It covers the global English exam. Exam prices and regional availability differ, so check CompTIA’s store for your country.

Key takeaways
✦ The two versions overlap for just under seven months: November 17, 2026 to June 11, 2027 (English).
✦ Both versions use the same format: up to 90 questions, 90 minutes, 750 to pass on a 100-900 scale.
✦ SY0-801 shifts weight toward General Security Concepts (12% to 16%) and Threats (22% to 24%), and cuts Security Program Management and Oversight from 20% to 14%.
✦ SY0-801 adds a dedicated objective on the security risks of artificial intelligence (AI). It is one topic among five domains, not the whole exam.
✦ Your testing date decides most of this. If you cannot be ready before June 11, 2027, plan for SY0-801.

According to CompTIA’s V8 page, SY0-801 launched on November 17, 2026. According to CompTIA’s V7 page, SY0-701 retires on June 11, 2027 in English and on August 13, 2027 in Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish and Thai. That leaves just under seven months when both English versions are available.

How to use the overlap window

  1. Estimate your weekly study hours honestly.
  2. Estimate the total hours you need. CompTIA lists 25 to 40 hours for its CertMaster Learn course and 10 to 20 hours for CertMaster Practice, and many candidates need more if they are new to IT.
  3. Divide, then add a buffer for a retake. At 8 hours a week, a 40-hour plan takes five weeks before any buffer.
  4. If your finish line lands after June 11, 2027, SY0-701 is off the table for English.

Does the exam version matter for your certification?

The version mostly affects what you study and when you test, not what you earn. Both exams lead to the CompTIA Security+ certification, and CompTIA’s Security+ credential is renewed through its continuing education process, generally on a three-year cycle. Retiring an exam version limits which exam new candidates can sit. It does not cancel a certification someone already holds.

Will employers prefer SY0-801 over SY0-701?

There is no official statement that employers rank one version above the other, so treat claims like that with caution. Read the exact wording in the job posting or contract. If it asks for “Security+,” the exam code is not part of the requirement. If it names a code, you have your answer.

What about DoD 8140?

CompTIA’s V7 page lists DoD 8140 work roles aligned to Security+ V7. CompTIA’s V8 page, as checked on November 18, 2026, did not list them. If your role falls under DoD 8140, confirm the approved-credential status of each version with your employer or the DoD’s own workforce resources before you choose.

What changed in SY0-801: domains, weights and new content

SY0-801 keeps the five-domain structure, but the weights and a few names changed. These figures come from CompTIA’s V7 and V8 exam objectives summaries.

The biggest additions and shifts

  • A dedicated AI risk topic. CompTIA’s V8 summary lists “Explain the security risks associated with artificial intelligence (AI)” in the Threats domain, covering risks to AI systems, models, prompts, privacy and data exposure. See our SY0-801 AI security scenarios for worked examples.
  • Mitigation moved. In V7, mitigation techniques sat in the Threats domain. In V8, the summary places “analyze indicators of vulnerabilities and implement appropriate mitigation strategies” in Security Operations.
  • Automation now mentions AI. The V8 automation and orchestration topic refers to AI-assisted workflows.
  • Less weight on governance. Program management drops six points, from 20% to 14%, so risk, compliance and policy questions should be a smaller share of your exam.
  • A new recommended experience profile. V8 lists a security administrator with two years of hands-on experience. V7 also listed Network+.
Pitfall: studying from a summary instead of the objectives.
Secondary sources reported different drafts of the SY0-801 objectives before launch, and some also claim specific V7 topics were dropped. Study from the official SY0-801 objectives (PDF) or the matching SY0-701 document, not from a blog’s summary, ours included.

Scenarios: which exam fits your situation?

Scenario A: you have already started SY0-701

If you can sit the exam before June 11, 2027, finishing SY0-701 is usually the efficient choice. Your notes, practice questions and pace all match the exam you will take. Add a short bridge on the V8 changes only if you might switch.

Scenario B: you are starting from zero

Without a deadline, start with SY0-801. It is the current blueprint, and building new habits on a version that retires within months gives you little. One caution: practice banks written for a brand-new exam may lag behind CompTIA’s own materials in the first weeks, so check that anything you buy says SY0-801 and was updated after the final objectives were published.

Scenario C: you have a hard deadline

A job offer or contract date beats every other factor. Book the version you can pass sooner, then confirm the requirement’s wording. Do not delay your attempt waiting for newer study material.

Scenario D: a specific version or DoD 8140 is required

Ask whoever owns the requirement, such as your hiring manager, contract officer or training office, in writing. Do not assume a new version is approved the day it launches.

Scenario E: your voucher was bought for SY0-701

Check the voucher’s expiration date and which exam versions it covers in your CompTIA account or with the seller. Then schedule well before June 11, 2027 so a rescheduling or a retake does not push you past the retirement date.

Switching from SY0-701 prep to SY0-801

Most of your 701 knowledge carries over, because both exams cover core security concepts, threats, architecture, operations and governance. If you decide to switch, work through these in order:

  1. Download the SY0-801 objectives and mark every objective you have not studied.
  2. Study the new AI risk topic until you can explain each risk and a matching defense in one sentence.
  3. Re-balance your time using the new weights: more General Security Concepts and Threats, less governance.
  4. Re-check where mitigation, automation and monitoring topics now sit in Security Operations.
  5. Take a timed practice set aligned to SY0-801 before booking.

Common mistakes when choosing a Security+ version

  • Assuming your certification expires when the exam retires. A retirement date applies to the exam, not to a certification you already earned.
  • Mixing materials from both versions. Notes built on one blueprint will leave gaps against the other, and the weights differ.
  • Waiting for “perfect” material. An extra month of waiting costs more than a small gap in practice questions.
  • Over-weighting the AI topic. It is one topic in the Threats domain. Security Operations is still the largest domain at 27%.
  • Booking too close to the retirement date. Leave room for a reschedule or a retake before June 11, 2027.
