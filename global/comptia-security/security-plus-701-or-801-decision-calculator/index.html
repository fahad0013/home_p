Security+ 701 or 801? Free Exam Decision Calculator

By Prostutii Editorial • Last Verified: 2026-11-18
⚡Direct Answer / Summary
Pick SY0-701 if you are well into 701 study and can test before it retires on June 11, 2027 (English). Pick SY0-801, launched November 17, 2026, if you are starting fresh or testing after that date. Use the calculator below to enter your progress and exam date and get a recommendation based on CompTIA’s published dates.

Last verified: November 18, 2026, against CompTIA’s official Security+ V7 and Security+ V8 pages. This calculator is independent guidance, not an official CompTIA tool.

Security+ has two live exam versions until June 2027, and the right one depends on where you are in your study and when you plan to test. The calculator below takes four answers, compares your dates with CompTIA’s published launch and retirement dates, and tells you which exam fits, with the reasons. It runs entirely in your browser.

Key takeaways
✦ Your planned exam date matters more than any other input. English SY0-701 is available until June 11, 2027.
✦ Both versions lead to the same Security+ certification, so the choice is about timing and study materials.
✦ If you are over 75% through SY0-701 study and can test before it retires, finishing it is usually fastest.
✦ If you are starting fresh, SY0-801 is the safer long-term choice.
✦ Leave at least 30 days between your exam date and the retirement date in case you must reschedule.

Two warnings can appear on top of any result. The first fires if your exam date is within 30 days of the retirement date, because a reschedule or retake could push you past it. The second fires if your exam date is later than your own credential deadline.

When to ignore the recommendation

Treat the result as a starting point. Override it in these cases:

  • A requirement names a version. If a job posting, contract or program names SY0-701 or SY0-801, follow it. If your role falls under DoD 8140, confirm approved-credential status with the requirement owner.
  • Your voucher is tied to a version. Check the voucher’s expiration date and which exam versions it covers before you commit.
  • Your exam date moves. Rebook, then run the calculator again with the new date.
  • CompTIA changes its dates or language offerings. The official pages above are the source of truth. If they differ from this page, trust them.
  • Test-center availability is tight. Slots near a retirement date fill up. Ask your testing provider about available dates before you rely on a late booking.
Pitfall: planning to test right at the deadline.
A candidate who books a SY0-701 slot a few days before retirement has no margin for illness, a canceled slot or a failed attempt. If your date is inside the 30-day buffer, move it earlier or choose SY0-801.
