Security+ 701 to 801: What to Add, What to Drop (2026)

By Prostutii Editorial • Last Verified: 2026-11-18
⚡Direct Answer / Summary
Most of your SY0-701 study carries over to SY0-801, because both exams use the same five domains and question format. The main additions are a dedicated AI risk objective and heavier weight on General Security Concepts (12% to 16%) and Threats (22% to 24%). Governance drops from 20% to 14%. Plan a one-to-two-week bridge, not a restart.

Last verified: November 18, 2026, against the exam objective summaries on CompTIA’s Security+ V7 and Security+ V8 pages. For sub-objective detail, use the official SY0-801 objectives (PDF).

You have been studying for SY0-701, and SY0-801 (Security+ V8) launched on November 17, 2026. This guide shows exactly what to add, what to re-weight, and what not to worry about, so you can decide whether to switch and, if you do, spend your time where it counts. If you have not chosen a version yet, start with our SY0-701 vs SY0-801 guide.

Key takeaways
✦ Both exams have five domains with the same format: up to 90 questions, 90 minutes, 750 to pass on a 100-900 scale.
✦ The biggest new item is a dedicated objective on the security risks of AI, listed under Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Attacks.
✦ Weights shift by +4, +2, +1, -1 and -6 points across the five domains. Security Operations is still the largest at 27%.
✦ Mitigation topics moved from the Threats domain into Security Operations.
✦ Governance (Domain 5) shrinks to 14% but is not gone. Roughly one question in seven still comes from it.
✦ If you can test on SY0-701 before it retires, you do not need to bridge at all.

How much of your SY0-701 prep carries over?

Most of it. CompTIA’s two exam summaries use the same five domain names, except that Domain 2 changed from “Mitigations” to “Attacks.” Domain 3 and Domain 5 list the same topic areas in both versions, and the exam format is identical. What changes is emphasis: one new AI objective, a bigger share for foundations and threats, a smaller share for governance, and a few topics that moved or were reworded.

As a rule of thumb, treat the switch as a gap-filling exercise. In our editorial estimate, a candidate who was close to exam-ready on SY0-701 needs roughly 8 to 12 focused hours to close the gaps below. That figure is a planning estimate, not a CompTIA number, so adjust it to your own practice scores.

Topics added or expanded in SY0-801

1. Security risks of artificial intelligence (new)

CompTIA’s V8 summary adds an objective on the security risks associated with AI, covering risks related to AI systems, models, prompts, privacy and data exposure. In plain terms: know what can go wrong when an organization uses AI, who or what is affected, and which control reduces each risk. Study tip: make a four-row table (system, model, prompt, data and privacy) and write one risk and one control for each. Our SY0-801 AI security scenarios walk through examples.

2. AI-assisted workflows in automation and orchestration

In V8, the automation and orchestration objective mentions AI-assisted workflows next to integrations and efficiency gains. Study tip: be able to explain why automation helps a security team (speed, consistency) and where a human should still review a decision (high-impact actions, ambiguous alerts).

3. Federation in identity and access

V8’s identity objective names federation alongside authentication, authorization and privileged access. Federation means one organization’s identity system vouches for a user to another organization’s systems, which is how single sign-on across companies works. Study tip: be able to distinguish authentication from authorization, and describe a scenario where federation reduces password sprawl.

4. Indicators of malicious activity and a renamed threats domain

Domain 2 is now “Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Attacks,” and one heading asks you to analyze indicators of malicious activity across malware, social engineering and credential attacks. Study tip: practice reading a short log or alert and naming the likely attack, because indicators are about evidence, not just definitions.

5. Mitigation moved into Security Operations

V8 places “analyze indicators of vulnerabilities and implement appropriate mitigation strategies” in Security Operations, where V7 had a mitigation heading in the Threats domain. Study tip: when you study a vulnerability, finish the loop: identify, prioritize, remediate, validate.

6. Heavier foundations

General Security Concepts rises from 12% to 16%, so control categories, change management and cryptography now carry more questions. Study tip: re-check certificates, hashing, digital signatures and key management, since V8’s summary names them together.

Common mistakes when upgrading

  • Restarting from zero. The domains and format are the same, so a full restart wastes weeks.
  • Overdoing the AI topic. It is one objective inside a 24% domain. Give it focused attention, not half your study time.
  • Neglecting governance. At 14% it is still about one in seven questions, and its risk concepts are some of the easiest points to earn with practice.
  • Mixing materials from both versions. The weights differ, so notes from one version will leave gaps against the other.
  • Switching when you do not need to. If you can sit SY0-701 before June 11, 2027, finishing it gives you the same Security+ certification. Use the decision calculator to check.
