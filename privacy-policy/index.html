03 · Authentication

Google Sign-In — how it works

Prostuti uses Sign in with Google as the only authentication method. There are no email/password accounts. This means:

🔑 The authentication flow
  • You tap "Sign in with Google" — Google's native picker opens on your device
  • Google authenticates you and returns a secure token (ID token) to our app
  • We pass that token to Supabase (our database), which verifies it with Google
  • Supabase creates a session — we store only your Google user ID, name, email, and photo URL
  • Your Google password is never sent to or stored by Prostuti

We receive from Google: your name, email address, and profile picture URL. We do not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail, Contacts, or any other Google service.

ℹ️ Google's privacy policy

Your use of Google Sign-In is also governed by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

To revoke Prostuti's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove "Prostuti" from third-party app access.

06 · Usage

How we use your data

We use the data we collect strictly for the following purposes:

  • Account management — creating and maintaining your Prostuti account using Google credentials
  • Exam delivery — serving MCQ questions, recording your answers, calculating scores and accuracy
  • Progress tracking — maintaining your roadmap progress, XP, streak, and daily study time
  • Personalisation — generating AI study recommendations based on your weak subjects and past performance
  • Leaderboard — displaying your display name, level, and XP rank publicly within the app to all Prostuti users
  • Notifications — sending study reminders and new content alerts (only if you have enabled notifications)
  • App improvement — using anonymised Firebase Analytics data to fix bugs and prioritise features
  • Legal compliance — retaining records as required by applicable law
🚫 We will never use your data to
  • Build advertising profiles about you to sell to third parties
  • Share your exam scores, mistakes, or study habits with employers or institutions
  • Train external AI models on your personal content without explicit consent
  • Send unsolicited marketing from third-party companies

🗑️ Account deletion

You can delete your Prostuti account at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Upon deletion, all personally identifiable data is permanently removed from our database within 30 days. Aggregated, anonymised analytics data (which cannot identify you) may be retained for longer.

You may also request deletion by emailing support.prostuti@gmail.com with your registered Google email address.

09 · Security

How we protect your data

  • Encryption in transit — all data between your device and our servers uses TLS 1.3
  • Encryption at rest — Supabase encrypts your data at rest using AES-256
  • Row-Level Security — Supabase RLS policies ensure users can only access their own data, not other users' records
  • No password storage — because we use Google Sign-In only, there are no passwords to protect or breach
  • Minimal permissions — the Android app requests only the permissions it needs: Internet access and notification permission (optional)
  • No third-party data brokers — we do not use any analytics, tracking, or data-sharing SDKs beyond those listed in this policy

Despite these measures, no system is 100% secure. If you discover a security issue, please report it responsibly to support.prostuti@gmail.com.

10 · Children

Children's privacy

👶 Age requirement

Prostuti is designed for students preparing for government job examinations in Bangladesh. These examinations require a minimum age of typically 18 years. Our app is therefore intended for users aged 18 and above.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years of age. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has submitted data, please contact us immediately.

AdMob (when enabled) will be configured with child-directed treatment flags set to false and will not serve personalised ads to users we identify as minors.
